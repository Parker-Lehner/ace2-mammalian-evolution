# Human Scientific Review Checklist

Ask the former professor and graduate student reviewers to focus on:

- Whether the main claim is sufficiently cautious.
- Whether BUSTED, aBSREL, PAML sensitivity, and enrichment evidence are separated clearly.
- Whether the title and abstract overstate ecological adaptation.
- Whether Q24/D30 are framed only as structural follow-up candidates.
- Whether the Methods provide enough detail to reproduce the analyses.
- Whether figures are understandable without claiming mechanisms not tested.
- Whether the paper is suitable for Journal of Molecular Evolution or should target a narrower journal.

Do not submit until reviewer comments have been addressed or explicitly declined with rationale.
